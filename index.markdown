# Thunderhead

**A consumer-friendly edge compute server. Your own thunderhead of a cloud — fully owned, fully controlled.**

The cloud doesn't have to live somewhere else. Thunderhead is a personal edge compute server that runs quietly in your home, integrating with your devices and your life — without asking for a subscription, a login, or your data.

Built on [DeviceNix](https://devicenix.com) and designed for [BenixOS](https://benixos.com) devices. Remote access powered by [QuickRing](https://quickring.me).
